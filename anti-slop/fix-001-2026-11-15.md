## Click-through (demo mode, Chromium)

- **Setup:** Next with empty fields shows both errors. Toggles respond to click and arrow keys. Courts + raises the count. Continue opens Players.
- **Players:** Add adds a name. Tapping two pair names swaps them and highlights the moved one. Shuffle reorders. Start opens the board.
- **Board:** Start match opens the scorer. Skip opens the confirm sheet: Tab stays inside, Escape closes it and focus returns to Skip, confirming skips the match and offers Undo. Share, Manage and Close session all act. The tabs switch to Standings and Live view.
- **Scorer:** the server picker sets who serves. Tapping a team adds a point (rolled number, flashed point). Undo works. The winning point shows the sweep and the confirm card, and Confirm returns to the board.
- **Second phone:** "That's me" claims a name. "Score my court" takes the court lock. A third phone sees "Sari is scoring this court" and a "Take over" button. The opponent confirms the result.
- **Roster:** Mark left with award updates the queue.
- **Close and summary:** Close session opens the summary and the sticker (Archivo, transparent). Switching to JKSL redraws the sticker in Jaksel. Dark mode reads correctly.
- **Other pages:** `/does-not-exist` shows the 404. Another phone opening `/s/:id` gets the permission page. `/p/:slug/tv` has no header controls, and Exit returns to the live view.

Console errors in all runs: none.
