## How this was checked

- Every screen opened in Chromium at 320×640, 360×640, 390×844, 820×1180 and 1280×800, light and dark: home, setup, players, courts board, scorer, standings, roster, live view, TV mode, join page.
- Overflow, tap-target size and clipped text measured in the browser on each screen and width.
- Keyboard: Tab order, focus ring, Escape and focus behaviour in sheets, arrow keys in option toggles.
- Contrast: every main colour pairing run through `contrast-check.py`, including the dimmed scoreboard.
- Text and code: scanned for em dashes, buzzwords, emoji, hardcoded English and comment patterns.

**Passed:** no horizontal overflow at any width from 320 to 1280 px. No console errors. No em dashes in UI text. No buzzwords, invented numbers, testimonials or fake logos. Both themes work. Every button has a real action. The last control on the board clears the bottom tab bar. Body text passes AA in both themes, including muted text (4.93:1 light, 8.17:1 dark).
